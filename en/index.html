The restaurant

A seafaring classic in Vilanova

La Gambarrada is a Mediterranean eating house on the Passeig del Carme, a couple of steps from the port. Seasonal produce, a wood fire and a menu that looks out to sea.

The kitchen starts from whatever the fish market brings in: wood-fired mussels with rosemary, baby squid, seafood rice and rock fish. The hand over the fire and the exact point of the broth are the house signature.

The dining room is bright and unhurried, with tables outside for sunny days. It works as well for a long family lunch as for dinner with friends.

Kitchen

What comes off the fire

The menu changes with the season and with whatever enters the fish market. These are the plates the house is best known for.

  • Wood-fired mussels with rosemary

    Over a wood fire with rosemary, and good bread to mop up.

  • House croquettes

    Creamy inside, crisp outside. They never last long.

  • Baby squid and squid

    Fresh, cooked just so, barely marked by the fire.

  • Seafood rice dishes

    Paella and soupy rice with produce from the fish market.

  • Tapas and small plates

    The easiest way to try half the menu without deciding.

  • Prawn carpaccio

    Cold, thin and dressed with good oil. A house classic.
